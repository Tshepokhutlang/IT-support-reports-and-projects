Issue Description:
VPN disconnected and won't reconnect

Business Impact:
Working remotely and lost VPN. Cannot access any internal resources.

Correct Solution Steps
1
Open the ticket and note this user is working remotely
2
In the Actions panel, click "Remote Support", then click "Connect" next to the user's computer
3
Open the Terminal app on the remote desktop
4
Run ipconfig /flushdns to clear stale DNS entries
5
Restart the remote computer to apply the changes
6
After the reboot, the PC asks for the user's sign-in password - ask them for it in Teams Chat to sign back in
7
Once signed in, open the VPN client and click Connect
8
Verify the VPN connects successfully - the tunnel should come back online
