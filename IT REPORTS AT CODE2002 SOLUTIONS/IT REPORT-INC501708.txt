Cannot download email attachments
Reported by: Beth Collins
Department: Sales | Location: Remote (Working from home)
Contact:-

Issue Description:
I can get emails fine but when I try to open the attachments nothing happens. Sometimes I get a weird error. Clients are sending me contracts I can't open.

Troubleshooting performed:
- Clicked the attachments a bunch
- Closed and reopened Mail

Business Impact:
Cannot access contracts and documents clients are sending. Work is blocked.

Correct Solution Steps
1
Open Tools → Remote Support and click Connect next to the user's PC
2
On the remote desktop, open Control Panel (search for it in the taskbar)
3
In Programs and Features, find "Mail Client" and click "Repair"
4
Wait for it to finish, then confirm attachments open

Why This Works
Attachment download failures usually indicate corrupted Mail configuration. Repairing the mail client through Control Panel resets temp folder permissions and attachment handling settings.
