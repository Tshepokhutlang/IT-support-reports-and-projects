Correct Solution Steps
1
Open Tools → Documentation → "Print Server" and note the correct printer IP
2
Open Tools → Remote Support and click Connect next to the user's PC
3
Open Settings → Devices
4
Find the offline printer and click Remove
5
Click "Add a printer or scanner" → "Add a printer using a TCP/IP address or hostname"
6
Enter the correct IP from the documentation
7
Click "Print Test Page", then confirm with the user that printing works
