Issue Description:
The clock on my computer is wrong! I'm in Central time but somehow my computer is set to Eastern. The minutes look off too by a couple. All my Teams meetings are showing up at the wrong times and I missed one already.

Troubleshooting performed by user:

Business Impact:
Missing meetings due to wrong timezone. File timestamps incorrect. Three more meetings scheduled this afternoon.

Correct Solution Steps
1
In the Actions panel (right side), click "Remote Support"
2
Click "Connect" next to the user's computer name
3
Open Settings → Time & Language
4
Change the timezone from Eastern to Central Time using the dropdown
5
Click "Sync Clock Now" to correct the time offset
6
Verify the clock and timezone are both correct
