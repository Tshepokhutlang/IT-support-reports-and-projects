Issue Description:
I came in this morning and my laptop is stuck on this blue screen asking for some kind of recovery key. I have no idea what that is or where to find it. I can't use my computer at all.

Troubleshooting performed:
- Blue screen wants some kind of recovery key
- I don't have the key

Business Impact:
User completely locked out of their computer. All work files inaccessible.

Correct Solution Steps
1
Open Tools → Directory and verify the caller's identity FIRST (Send Verification Code, confirm Employee ID / Manager)
2
If they fail verification, do NOT release the key — close the ticket. Refusing is the correct security action
3
Once verified, look up the user in Directory to find their Drive Encryption recovery key
4
Open Tools → Remote Support and click Connect next to the locked computer
5
Enter the recovery key on the Drive Encryption screen to unlock the drive
6
Confirm Workstation OS boots without the recovery prompt
