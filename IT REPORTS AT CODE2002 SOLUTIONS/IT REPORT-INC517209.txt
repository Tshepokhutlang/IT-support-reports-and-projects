Internet keeps cutting in and out on Floor 2
Reported by: Andrew Kim 
Department: Sales | Location: Remote (Working from home)
Contact:-

Issue Description:
Users on Floor 2 report their network connection drops for 30 seconds every few minutes then reconnects. Affects both wired and wireless. Pattern started this morning.

Troubleshooting performed:
- Multiple people on Floor 2 having the same issue
- Other floors seem fine

Business Impact:
Video calls keep dropping. Cloud-based work constantly losing connection.

Correct Solution Steps
1
Open Tools → Server Room
2
Open the "Devices" tab and find "Floor 2 Switch"
3
Click its Power button to reboot it
4
Ask the user over Team Chat if the connection is stable — it resolves when they confirm

Why This Works
Intermittent drops affecting an entire floor point to the floor switch experiencing spanning-tree reconvergence or a firmware bug. Rebooting the switch clears the fault and restores stable connectivity.
