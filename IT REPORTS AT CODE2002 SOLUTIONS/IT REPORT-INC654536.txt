Mail keeps asking for password repeatedly
Reported by: David Lee
Department: Customer Support | Location: Floor 1
Contact:-

Issue Description:
Mail keeps asking me for my password over and over. I type it in, it works for a minute, then it asks me again. I'm typing the right password.

Troubleshooting performed:
- Typed my password many times
- Checked caps lock is off

Business Impact:
Cannot reliably access email. Missing important communications.

Correct Solution Steps
1
Open Tools → Remote Support and click Connect next to the user's PC
2
On the remote desktop, open Control Panel (search for it in the taskbar)
3
In Programs and Features, find "Mail Client" and click "Repair"
4
Wait for it to finish, then confirm the password prompts stop

Why This Works
Repeated password prompts in Mail usually indicate corrupted cached credentials. Repairing the mail client through Control Panel resets the credential cache and mail profile.
