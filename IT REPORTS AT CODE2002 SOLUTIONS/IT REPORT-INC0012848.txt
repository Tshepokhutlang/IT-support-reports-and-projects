Cannot get to my files on the shared drive anymore
Reported by: Sarah Mitchell
Department: Marketing | Location: Remote (Working from home)
Contact: x4892

Issue Description:
I can't get to my files on the Marketing shared drive today. I keep getting "The network path was not found" when I try to open it. I'm working from home today. My internet and email both work fine.

Troubleshooting performed by user:
- Restarted my computer (no change)
- Internet and email work normally
- The mapped drives say disconnected

Business Impact:
Unable to access marketing campaign files. Deadline for Q1 campaign materials is EOD tomorrow.

Correct Solution Steps
1
In the Actions panel (right side), click "Remote Support"
2
Click "Connect" next to the user's computer name to start a remote session
3
On the remote desktop, check the VPN Client - it shows DISCONNECTED (the user is on WiFi without VPN, so drives can't reach the file server)
4
Open the "VPN Client" app on the remote desktop and click the green "Connect" button
5
Once VPN is connected, click "Documentation" in the Actions panel and open the "File Server" article to find the correct UNC path for the Marketing department
6
On the remote desktop, open File Explorer → click "This PC" → click the ⋯ (See more) button in the top bar → "Map network drive"
7
In the dialog, select drive letter D:, enter the UNC path from the documentation, and click "Map Drive"
